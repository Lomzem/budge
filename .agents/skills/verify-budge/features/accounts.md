# Accounts, held Accounts and starting balances

Create Cash and Credit Card Accounts, arrange them, and decide where an unknown synced bank Account's rows go.

## Sub-features

- `account-create`, `account-start`: Cash/Card creation and cutover starting Transaction.
- `account-start-action`: Account actions → Starting balance shows the current amount and replaces it. The field starts filled with the current amount when it's confirmed (always for an Account no connection feeds, and for a fed one once its Starting Balance is confirmed); an unconfirmed one starts empty, with no placeholder, unless the bank's estimate fills it.
- `account-start-edit`: editing a Starting Balance offers only Date, Category, Direction, Amount and Memo.
- `account-rename`, `account-order`, `account-missing`: names, group order and invalid route.
- `account-held`, `account-suggested`: map/create/ignore held bank Accounts and confirm the Starting Balance step.
- `account-setup-confirmed`: each bank Account a connection feeds stays `Starting balance not confirmed` on its Bank card until the owner confirms that Account's Starting Balance for the current cutover date (changing the date unconfirms it); the step's `What it’s based on` names the bank Account the server asks for the balance (of those mapped to the Account, the one Budge recorded last) and says what Budge can't know. A bank Account set up anew in another Account keeps `Its earlier Transactions are still in <old Account>. Only new ones come to <Account>.` on its row until each of them is moved or deleted: in warning text while its Starting Balance is unconfirmed, then as muted information with an info icon once confirmed.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Desktop sidebar `Add Account` beside the Accounts heading; on every width, the Accounts page's `Add Account`.
- Named Account page → `<name> actions` → Rename, Move up, Move down, Starting balance, and one `Stop syncing <bank Account label>` per bank Account that feeds it (its result `Stopped syncing <label>. Its Transactions stay in <Account>. Bring it back from the Bank page.` floats at the bottom of the screen with `Dismiss`, which returns focus to the actions button; see [plaid.md](plaid.md)). The header reads the Account name (h1) first, then its type, then `getByTestId("account-sync")`, which names the Bank Connection that feeds it (see [plaid.md](plaid.md)).
- Below `lg`, the Accounts page folds the Account list into a `Your Accounts <count>` disclosure. It starts closed, so the register is in view, and a plain visit opens it only if the owner left it open last time (remembered in this browser); arriving with a filter in the URL keeps it closed.
- Bank page → the connection's card → `New bank Accounts` region → `Set up <name> ending <mask>` (the row shows the name, `Ending <mask>` on its own line, then `<n> transactions waiting`, or `No transactions waiting yet` for one just brought back) → dialog `<name> ending <mask>` → radio `Into <Account>` / `Into a new Account` / `Nowhere: ignore this bank Account` → `Import its transactions` or `Ignore` → dialog `Starting balance for <Account name>` → `Confirm`, `Skip` or `Suggest again`. A confirm here or from `Check balance` floats `<Account>'s starting balance is confirmed. Categorize its Transactions` at the bottom of the Bank page; the link opens `/accounts?account=<id>&category=uncategorized`. When the Account's bank Account still has earlier Transactions in another Account, it floats `… is confirmed. Its bank Account's earlier Transactions are still in <old Account>. Open <old Account>` instead, linking to that Account's page. Focus never falls to the page when the dialog's opener is gone: after `Confirm` it lands on the floated link; after `Skip` or Escape on the Starting balance step it lands on that bank Account's `Check balance` on the card, or the card itself when none remains; after `Ignore` it lands on the card. Escape or Cancel while the opener remains returns to it.
- Bank page → a card's `Where <connection> goes` list: one row per fed Account, `<Account> from <bank Account label>`; an unconfirmed row adds `Starting balance not confirmed` and the button `Check <Account>'s starting balance` (text `Check balance`), which opens the same Starting balance dialog. A row whose bank Account's earlier Transactions are still in another Account adds `Its earlier Transactions are still in <old Account>. Only new ones come to <Account>.`, the old Account a link to its page. Each unconfirmed row counts once toward the Bank's `need you` badge and the Budget's `Bank: <n> things need you`; the earlier-Transactions note alone counts nowhere.
- In the Starting balance dialog, the description names the cutover date (`Budge records it on <Mon> 1, <year>, the cutover date.`; a card's field is `Amount owed on <date>`). The closed disclosure `What it’s based on` (only for an Account a connection feeds) lists the bank Account and connection, `Balance now, says the bank` (`getByTestId("bank-balance")`: the bank's figure, or `Unknown. <reason>`), `When the bank measured it` (always `Unknown. Not available to Budge.`), `Imported since <date>` (`getByTestId("imported-since")`: `None` or `<n> Transactions, dated <first> to <last>`), their net amount when there are any, and `Every Transaction since <date> arrived?` (always unknown), then says a working connection doesn't prove the balance. `Confirm` with the field empty keeps the dialog open, focuses the field and says beneath it `Enter the balance your bank showed on <date>.` (a card: `Enter what you owed on <date>.`) as the field's description; a bank estimate that arrives after the field was filled with a confirmed amount leaves it, saying `Budge estimates <amount>. The confirmed balance stays.`

## Gotchas

- The `bank-sync` fixture builds held Accounts and rows through the production sync mutations, with no Plaid. Its seeded mappings (Checking from Sample Everyday Checking, Savings from Sample Statement Savings) start unconfirmed, like every mapping until its Account's Starting Balance is confirmed.
- A Starting Balance is a Cleared Transaction with `kind: 'startingBalance'`; the server refuses moving it, giving it a Payee, splitting it or making it a Transfer, and the dialog hides those edits. Card debt uses the opposite sign from Cash. Sources: tickets 03, 10, 21; public `accounts.list/create/rename/reorder/setStartingBalance/startingBalance`, `heldAccounts.list/map/createAccount/ignore/suggestStartingBalance/startingBalanceEvidence`. Ticket 02 of first-use-ux added the confirmation and the evidence.
