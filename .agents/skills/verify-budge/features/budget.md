# Budget

Give money a job by editing a Category's Assigned amount and see Ready to Assign change. Move between months without losing the saved assignment.

## Sub-features

- `budget-entry`: direct route, desktop sidebar, and the phone tab bar reach Budget.
- `budget-assign`: adding $10 to Groceries lowers Ready to Assign by $10.
- `budget-persist`: reload retains Assigned and Ready to Assign.
- `budget-month`: next then previous month preserves the original month's Assigned; Ready to Assign is global. Away from the current month a `This month` button (icon only below `sm`, hidden below `xs`) returns to it; in the current month its place stays reserved but invisible, so the row never shifts.
- `budget-mobile`: below `sm` the table shows Category, Assigned and Available (one row per Category with its status and Target bar under the name; a card's `waiting on` notes show only from `sm`, and the inspector lists them under Card payment); down to 320 px Assigned stays an editable field in the table; the money columns follow the table's own width, not the screen's: under 28rem (448 px) of table, as on a phone or at 1024 px beside the sidebar and the inspector, the Available column folds under Assigned (header `Assigned` over `Available`, each row's and group's Available stacked under its Assigned), from 28rem Available has its own column and from 36rem (576 px) Activity does too; an Assigned of $999,999.99, its Available and seven-figure Group totals read in full at 320, 390, 1024 and 1280 px; a Target's `Assign $X to <name>` button shows under an overspent Category too, and the Category's inspector dialog (`Assigned this month`) keeps its header with Close pinned while it scrolls. It opens, and `Previous`/`Next Category` reopen it, scrolled to the top, and those buttons truncate a long name on one line (the full name is in their label). Nothing overflows at 320 px. The `Needs you` chips use short text below `sm` (`N overspent`, `Bank: N to do`). Ready to Assign is a full-width row with its label on the left and the amount (never wrapped) on the right, and any Uncategorized note on its own reserved line beneath, and `Needs you` is a fixed-height strip of chips that scrolls sideways instead of wrapping; from `sm` the chips wrap onto more lines instead.
- `budget-negative`: assigning more than Ready to Assign shows the negative Ready to Assign warning.
- `budget-groups`: each Category Group header row totals its shown and Hidden Categories' Assigned, Activity and Available.
- `budget-collapse`: each group header is a button (`aria-expanded`) that collapses its Categories; the header keeps its totals and, when any is overspent, `N overspent`. The choice is kept per browser (localStorage), and opening a Category in a collapsed group (by `Needs you` or `Previous`/`Next Category`) expands it.
- `budget-month-jump`: the month heading is a button (`<Month Year>, choose a month`) that opens a year grid with `Previous year`/`Next year`; arrow keys move within the grid, months before the cutover month are disabled, and the arrows keep working. Each month change replaces the current history entry with a real navigation (`keepInUrl` in `src/lib/keepInUrl.ts`), so Back from another Section returns to the month viewed last, even after a reload. A history entry without `?month` is the current month: Back or Forward to one shows it, while a link into the Budget without a month keeps the month viewed last.
- `budget-target-assign`: a Category whose Target needs money shows `Assign $X to <name>` in its row.
- `budget-carry`: a Category's Available rolls forward into the next month.
- `budget-status`: `Funded` names a Target that is met; a Category without a Target shows no status for a positive Available (only its green amount).
- `budget-review-links`: the `Needs you` chips: `N Uncategorized`, `N Unapproved` and `N bank changes` (`500+ bank changes` past the newest 500) link into the Accounts page already filtered (the last to Status `Bank changes`); `N Categories overspent` opens the first overspent Category's inspector. The Budget waits for every count before it paints, so chips never arrive one at a time; a strip that overflows fades at the clipped edge.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `/` directly after sign-in.
- Choose `Budget` in the desktop sidebar or the phone tab bar.
- The month is an h2 (holding the month picker button) between `Previous month` and `Next month`; below `sm` `Edit Categories` is an icon button at the end of that row whose menu offers `Add Category Group` and `Add Category` (Credit Card Payments can never take a Category; Add Category can also make a new group).
- From Budget, the `Needs you` region's `… Uncategorized` and `… Unapproved` links lead to `/accounts?category=uncategorized` and `/accounts?status=unapproved`; the `… overspent` chip is a button that opens the inspector.

## Driving it with Playwright

Journeys (`bun run app verify <name>` reseeds the journey's fixture, drives it, and keeps its evidence; the sources are `e2e/journeys/budget.ts` and `e2e/journeys/shell.ts`, also run by `bun run test:e2e:local`):

- `budget/assign-back`: while an Assigned save is held, focusing the field shows the pending amount; typing the old amount back saves it, and the old amount and Ready to Assign survive a reload once the held save lands.
- `budget/assign`: `budget-assign`, `budget-persist`, `budget-month` in the current month; Enter saves and moves to the next Category's Assigned field (the last one stays put); invalid text shows a short hint below the field, aligned to its right edge (at 390 px the journey checks it is scrolled clear of the tab bar and the next Category's row), and the screen-reader alert exists only while there is an error; ArrowDown/ArrowUp move between Assigned fields (saving a changed amount first; an invalid one keeps focus).
- `budget/needs-you`: `budget-review-links`: the Uncategorized and Unapproved chips land on a filtered register, and the overspent chip opens the first overspent Category; each further click steps to the next one, scrolling its row to the middle of the screen. `Needs you` lists the over-assigned and overspent chips first, then the Bank chip (red when a connection needs a reconnect or failed), then the register chips.
- `budget/month-jump`: `budget-month-jump` at 320 px: two months back (the first budget month) is enabled; `Previous year` stops at the first budget year and three months back is disabled when it falls in that year; `Next year` then a month a year ahead, kept by a reload; `Previous month` steps back one; picking a month closes the grid and returns focus to the month button; from June next year, ArrowLeft, ArrowDown and Enter pick August (an arrow pressed straight after reopening keeps its move); after a reload on August, picking September and saving its Groceries Assigned, Categories then Back shows September with that amount, Forward then Back still September, and `Previous month` shows August's own amount; from a fresh `/`, Categories then Budget by tab, `Next month` with its own Groceries Assigned, then Back twice shows the current month with its own amount and no `?month`, still after a reload, and Forward twice returns to next month.
- `budget/large-amounts`: Groceries Assigned $999,999.99 saved at 390 px and reloaded; at 390, 320, 1024 (beside the sidebar and inspector) and 1280 px, Groceries' Available and the Everyday group's seven-figure Assigned and Available each show exactly once in full, no table cell or Assigned field is cut short (`expectEveryAmountShownInFull`), and nothing passes the right edge.
- `budget/phone-groups`: at 320 px on a touch screen a Group's `Add Category to <group>` button takes taps across 44 px; Assigned is edited in the table and survives a reload, and group totals show Assigned over Available; `budget-collapse`: a group with an overspent Category collapses, keeps its totals, shows `N overspent` (N its overspent Categories), stays collapsed after a reload, and the overspent chip expands it.
- See also `shell/navigate` in [shell.md](shell.md): `budget-entry` and phone navigation/overflow only.

Preconditions for the manual recipes: baseline fixture and authenticated `page`; start in the current month at `/`, with the `Budget` H1 and `getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Assigned for Groceries' })`.

- **Negative warning (manual):** fill Groceries Assigned with Ready to Assign plus $1 more than its current amount and press Enter. Require `getByRole('status').filter({ hasText: 'Ready to Assign is negative' })` naming $1.00 over. Restore and require the warning gone.
- **Group totals (manual):** scope `getByRole('rowgroup').filter({ hasText: 'Credit Card Payments' })` style per group; require its header row's Assigned, Activity (desktop only) and Available to equal the sum of all its Categories, including Hidden Categories outside the table. Change one Assigned and require the header total to move by the same amount.
- **Carry-forward (manual):** record Groceries Available, click `Next month`, and require next month's Available to start from that amount plus next month's Assigned and Activity.
- **Review links (manual):** on the seeded Budget, require the `N Uncategorized` and `… Unapproved` links when the fixture has such rows; click each and require the `Accounts` heading with the Category filter at `Uncategorized` (or Status at `Unapproved`) and only matching rows. When a shown Category is overspent, click `N Categories overspent` and require the `Category inspector` region for the first one. If the fixture has none, the link is absent by design: record the journey as blocked by fixture, not passed.

## Gotchas

- **Manual drive:** at 320 px edit `Assigned for Groceries` in the table or `Assigned this month` in its inspector dialog; blur and Enter each save, Escape puts back the saved amount, sums such as `100+20` save their total, and invalid text keeps the draft and announces alert `Enter an amount, such as 120.00 or 100+20. Escape puts back the saved amount.` and shows `Try 120.00, 100+20 or +5. Esc restores.` under the field, pushing the rows below down rather than covering them, without changing the saved amount. At `lg` and up, the side panel says `Select a Category.` until a Category is open. On a phone, `Edit Categories` is a menu of Add Group, Add Category and Manage Categories. Reload to prove persistence.
- **Manual drive:** navigate back to the cutover month and require `Previous month` disabled. With suitable Transactions and hidden Categories, verify `includes … Uncategorized` under `Ready to Assign`, and `Hidden Categories` with Available amounts; a negative hidden amount is red with the screen-reader label `Overspent`. An overspent visible Category shows in `Needs you` and in its own row, not in a separate list. The hidden list has no Category-opening buttons.

- The viewed month is in the URL as `?month=YYYY-MM` (dropped for the current month), so reload, Back and a shared link keep it. The recipe edits the current month.
- Ready to Assign is global, including future assignments. Do not assert it equals only this month's income.
- Wait for reactive controls and persisted values; a changed input by itself proves only a draft.
