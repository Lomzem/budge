# Inspector, move money and Cover

Inspect a Category, move Assigned between jobs, and cover Overspending from Ready to Assign.

## Sub-features

- `inspect-open`: keyboard opening/closing on desktop and phone.
- `move-money`: an explicit From/To form (this Category on one side, a searchable Category picker on the other, a `Swap From and To` button) that preserves Ready to Assign. Once anything is chosen, typed or refused, a `Clear` button empties the form and focuses the picker, and Escape inside the form does the same instead of closing the inspector, in the side panel and in the phone dialog alike; a clean form's Escape closes as before. A done move is announced politely (`Moved $10.00 from Groceries to Coffee.`), naming the From, To and amount as submitted, even if the form was swapped or retyped while it was on its way.
- `cover`: cover exactly the negative Available from Ready to Assign, or `Cover from another Category`, which fills the shortfall into the move form with this Category as To. A done cover is announced politely with the amount the server covered (`Covered $88.29 for Dining Out from Ready to Assign.`), which can exceed the button's amount if Available fell meanwhile.
- `inspect-manage`: rename the Category in place from the header, and its `<name> actions` menu (Rename, Move to group, Hide, Delete…).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Budget table → Category row or Category-name button, or the `Needs you` chip `N Categories overspent`, which opens the first overspent Category (each further click the next). Restore hidden Categories from the `Hidden` section before opening them.
- At 1024 px and up the inspector sits in a sticky side panel that scrolls on its own; opening it focuses the region without scrolling the page. Below that it opens as a dialog whose `Assigned this month` row is an editable amount field (the table keeps its Assigned field too, even at 320 px), with an `Other Categories` nav of `Previous Category: <name>` and `Next Category: <name>` buttons (the visible name truncates on one line) that switch the dialog to the neighboring Category, scrolled to the top, and focus its Assigned field. The dialog opens scrolled to the top, with Ready to Assign in view.

## Driving it with Playwright

Journeys (`bun run app verify <name>` reseeds the journey's fixture, drives it, and keeps its evidence; the source is `e2e/journeys/inspector.ts`, also run by `bun run test:e2e:local`):

- `inspector/move-money`: `inspect-open` and `move-money` by keyboard, a side panel that takes no input while the next month loads (sync held by `pausableSync` in `e2e/heldResponse.ts`), and the inspector as a dialog at 320 px that Close and Escape return to the Category and whose `Next Category` steps to the neighbor with its Assigned field focused. A click on another Category while the inspector's ⋯ menu is open only closes the menu and keeps a started move; at 320 px a click on the dialog's Move button does the same and moves nothing, and a touch tap 2 px left of the open menu, level with `Move down`, only closes the menu and moves no Category. An open menu puts a transparent dismiss layer (`src/lib/components/menu-dismiss-layer.svelte`) over the page and dialogs, so an outside click or tap lands on it, and Chromium's touch adjustment picks it over a nearby menu item. An Escape as the inspector's Delete dialog opens closes only Delete. The move form starts with this Category as To; `Swap From and To` flips it, typing `cof` in the picker leaves one option, a move is announced in the inspector's status; a move held in transit (`pausableSync`) keeps the From, To and amount it was sent with through a swap, announces those, and keeps an amount typed meanwhile; an empty Amount is refused, and Escape then `Clear` each empty a started move and focus the picker without closing the inspector; at 320 px, Escape clears a started move with a chosen Category, typed Amount and refusal while the dialog stays open, and a second Escape closes it back to Groceries.
- `inspector/cover-and-manage`: at 1280 px cover the first overspent Category from the Category with the most Available (the Amount is prefilled with exactly the shortfall; afterwards its Available is $0.00 and the donor's falls by exactly the shortfall), leaving Ready to Assign unchanged after a reload, and the move announced; then, with this tab's server frames held by `pausableSync` while a second tab lowers the next overspent Category's Assigned by $5.00, `Cover $X from Ready to Assign` on the stale tab covers the server's shortfall: it announces `Covered $(X + 5.00) for <name>…`, leaves Available $0.00, Assigned at the stale Assigned plus X, and Ready to Assign lowered by exactly X, also after a reload; a Groceries rename refused by `failFirstCallTo` reopens with its reason and draft, and the draft, left by a fast click on Coffee, renames Groceries (not Coffee, which opens unedited with focus on its inspector) after a reload; at 320 px with touch a name typed for that Category and left by a CDP tap on `Next Category: Dining Out` renames it, not Dining Out (which opens unedited with Assigned focused), after a reload on Budget; rename it again in place by Enter, move it to a collapsed Savings Goals (the group opens to show it and focus stays on its actions), hide it (focus goes to Car Repairs, the Category before it, and it lists under `Hidden`), a Transit Hide held by `holdResponseTo` and answered after Coffee opened with a name being typed hides Transit and leaves Coffee's inspector, draft and focus, and delete Dining Out into Coffee (focus goes to Coffee, the Category after it, and Coffee's Activity and Assigned gain Dining Out's after a reload), all from the inspector.

The recipes below cover what the journeys leave out.

Preconditions:

- Authenticated baseline from the index; reseed before independent mutations.

- **Inspect:** focus `getByRole("button", { name: "Groceries", exact: true })`, press Enter; require `getByRole("region", { name: "Groceries", exact: true })` visible and Left over from last month visible; opening focuses the region (the side panel at ≥1024 px, the dialog below). Escape from inside the region or Close Groceries must restore focus. Repeat at 320 px: it opens as `getByRole("dialog", { name: "Groceries" })` scrolled to the top, and Close or Escape returns focus to the Category-name button.
- **Move:** record Assigned for Groceries/Coffee and Ready to Assign; in region click `Swap From and To` so Groceries is From, open the `To` picker, type `Coffee` and choose it (focus moves to Amount), fill Amount 10.00, submit `Move` (in the `Move money` section). Require Assigned −$10/+ $10 and Ready to Assign unchanged; reload and reopen. Swap back to reverse and verify.
- **Cover (journeyed in `inspector/cover-and-manage`; repeat by hand only for a Cash Category):** create an invented outflow large enough to overspend an ordinary Cash Category using Transaction UI; select it on Budget. Click its `Cover $… from Ready to Assign` action (inspect actual amount in ARIA). Require Available exactly zero, Assigned increased by deficit and Ready to Assign decreased by deficit; reload.

## Gotchas

- **Manual drive:** check the Target surface on ordinary Categories (Add/Edit Target, progress and Assign when needed) and `Card payment` on Credit Card Payment Categories.
- Hiding or deleting the open Category from its inspector closes the inspector and puts focus on the Category beside it: the next one in its group, else the previous one, else its group, else the main region. Moving it to a collapsed group opens that group, keeps the inspector open, and retains focus on the actions button.
- **Manual drive:** the From/To picker includes hidden Categories and excludes the current Category. Zero, negative or invalid Amount shows `Enter an amount above zero, such as 25.00.`; a positive move may make the source Assigned negative while preserving Ready to Assign.

- A touch probe must turn on touch emulation (`useATouchScreen`) before it measures a menu: touch emulation removes the scrollbar and moves a centered dialog and its menu about 12 px. A point measured before that can land inside the menu.
- Under an open menu, Playwright's `locator.click()` on a page element times out because the dismiss layer covers it. Use `clickTheSpotOf` (`e2e/journeys/journey.ts`) to click the point the user sees.
- Cover requires actual negative Available; if fixture has no negative Category create Overspending via UI or report blocked, not passed by opening an inspector.
