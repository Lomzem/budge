**Gaps I assessed without raising a finding:**

- **G3:** the deleted-Category Undo sentence is truthful and readable.
- **G6:** a superseded successful sync leaves only the newer action's report. When the user's last deliberate act was Stop syncing or Bring back, silence about the older sync is reasonable, and its rows still show in the registers and in freshness. A superseded failure stays visible.
- **G7:** the sync reports count only additions ("No Transactions added.", "N Transactions added."), which is truthful for what they claim.
